Cat Stack Sort: Color Puzzle

Mobile game · Puzzle · App Store live

Square cats are stubborn: they only sit with cats of their own color. Tap to move them between cardboard boxes and seal every full box with “MEOW” tape. Explore 500 solver-checked levels across five series. The first 5 levels of every series are free; one purchase unlocks everything. No ads.
