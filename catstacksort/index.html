What is it?

Square cats are stubborn: they only want to sit with cats of their own color. Tap a box to pick up the top cats and tap another to drop them in. Fill a box with four matching cats and it closes with a satisfying "MEOW" tape. Every one of the 500 levels is checked by a solver, so every puzzle can be beaten.
