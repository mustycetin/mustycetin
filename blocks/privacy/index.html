Blocks: Puzzle Game — Privacy Policy

Developer: Creacet Software · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Summary

Blocks: Puzzle Game does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share any personal data. The game has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no user accounts.

Data stored on your device

Your settings (theme, language, music, sound effects, vibration), best scores, your unfinished Endless game, your daily free-play time and whether you own the Full Version are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes this data. We cannot see or access it.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Full Version" purchase is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never receive your payment details. The game only asks the store whether the Full Version is owned on your account.

Permissions

Internet/network access is used only to talk to the app store for purchases and restores. Vibration is used for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: mustycetin@gmail.com
